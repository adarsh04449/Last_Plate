Last Plate - Art credits

The kitchen background, chef poses, customer portraits, and ingredient images
were generated for this project using OpenAI image generation.

The images are in Assets/Resources/Art. GameBootstrapper.cs cuts the character
sheets into sprites and loads the separate ingredient images. The earlier
character_ingredient_sheet.png is kept as a fallback.

The plate and plain UI panels are drawn in code. No purchased art packs are used.
