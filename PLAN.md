# Last Plate - Remaining work

The catching game is the version to finish. It uses one kitchen screen, one order
at a time, and a two-minute round. No more chopping or cooking screens are planned.

## Finished

- Movement, falling food, and ordered recipes.
- Three-slot plate, carry weight, and tilt with rechargeable energy.
- Burnt food damage, three lives, and three difficulty stages.
- Customer patience, golden ingredients, combos, and results.
- Kitchen art, chef poses, customer portraits, sounds, and quiet music.
- Start, pause, and restart screens.
- Fixes for repeated tilt starts, the energy meter, and repeat scoring.
- 15 gameplay tests and the Game scene in the build list.

## Before submission

- Play a full round using both keyboard control schemes.
- Check whether late-stage difficulty and customer patience feel fair.
- Check voice, music, and effect volume on speakers and headphones.
- Try the standalone build on the computer used for the recording.
- Record a run showing correct catches, a wrong catch, tilt recovery, burnt food,
  a difficulty change, pause, and the results screen.
- Push the final changes to GitHub.

## Possible later additions

A saved high score or a short first-play tutorial could be useful. These are
optional; keeping the current game clear and working comes first.
