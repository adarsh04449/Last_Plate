Human-recorded performances, used at the original pitch and speed.
Selected files are renamed for their in-game purpose:
  no0.wav, no1.wav -> chef_damage_01.wav, chef_damage_02.wav
  yes0.wav, yes1.wav -> chef_golden_01.wav, chef_golden_02.wav
  victory0.wav, victory1.wav -> chef_complete_01.wav, chef_complete_02.wav

Unity imports these short WAV files as mono PCM and normalizes their level.
Reactions alternate between two takes per event. Completion takes priority over a
golden catch; burnt-food reactions take priority over both. Arcade effects lower
in volume during speech. There is no background music.

The original macOS text-to-speech AIFF clips were replaced. The reactions use the
actor's existing negative, positive, and victory performances rather than the
previous synthesized phrases (Oh no / Nice catch / Order up).

Other arcade effects: generated by Last Plate's AudioManager at runtime.
