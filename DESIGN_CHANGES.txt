Last Plate - Changes from the original design

The original plan was a 60-second game where wrong ingredients cost a life.
The main idea is still catching a three-ingredient recipe and tilting the plate,
but a few things changed during development.

1. The round is now two minutes long. Each difficulty stage lasts 40 seconds,
   giving the player more time to learn the controls before Kitchen Chaos.

2. Only burnt food removes lives. Wrong ingredients stay on the plate and can be
   discarded with Space. This makes mistakes recoverable.

3. The plate holds three ingredients, shown above the chef. Carrying more food
   slows movement slightly. Tilt uses energy, which recharges after Space is
   released.

4. Orders use five named recipes and one customer at a time. Customer types have
   different patience times and bonuses. A shrinking bar shows the time left.
   An expired order is replaced without taking a life.

5. The next required ingredient appears regularly rather than leaving every
   spawn completely random. The order also highlights what to catch next.

6. Scoring includes catch points, combos, golden ingredients, and customer
   bonuses. Catch points can only be earned once for each slot on an order.
   Results show score, orders served, best combo, and a chef rank.

7. Separate chopping and cooking screens were tried and then removed. The game
   went back to one kitchen screen to keep the controls and goal easier to follow.

8. Art was added for the kitchen, chef, food, and customers. The chef changes pose
   after damage and completed orders. Artwork source notes are in README.md.

9. The original plan had no music. The current version has quiet background music
   that gets softer during voice reactions. Music can be switched off with M.
   The voices and music come from CC0 packs credited in README.md.

10. The project includes 15 automated gameplay tests and a saved build scene.
    These check issues such as repeated damage, tilt running out, replacement
    orders, scoring the same ingredient slot twice, pause, and restart.
